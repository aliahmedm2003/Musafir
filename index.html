📱 SMS Marketing for Local Businesses

Create marketing messages that reach tourists. Works even with bad internet.

💡 How it works:
1. Restaurant creates SMS message (offline)
2. Message queues locally
3. When restaurant has internet, click "Sync & Send" → messages queue in SMS gateway
4. Gateway sends via cellular network to all tourists in that region
5. Tourists receive SMS directly to their phone (no internet needed for them)

Cost: 5 PKR per SMS sent
